3. LINKEDIN POST (Operations & Supply Chain Leaders)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Who gets the inventory when stock runs out?"

Every operations and warehouse director knows this nightmare scenario:
You have 40 units of a critical industrial gauge in stock. Three tier-1 clients place competing orders totaling 75 units on the same morning.

If your sales team argues over who shouts loudest, you risk contractual penalties, damaged relationships, and costly partial shipments.

OrderWise replaces spreadsheet guesswork with an explainable allocation engine:

1. Deterministic Multi-Pass Allocation: Allocates down to the unit based on approved priority, promised dates, and partial-fulfillment tolerances.
2. Human-in-the-Loop AI: Leverages LLMs to evaluate order urgency from qualitative field notes, while requiring authorized manager approval before any inventory row is reserved.
3. Impact Analyzer: Compare allocation runs side-by-side to understand exact revenue and order impact before dispatch.
