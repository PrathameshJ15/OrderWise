# Brag Plan: OrderWise — 25s Vertical Instagram Reel

## What is this app?
OrderWise is an enterprise-grade AI inventory allocation and supply chain intelligence platform that resolves competing customer orders across scarce inventory using deterministic business rules, governed AI priority recommendations, and immutable audit explanations.

## The angle
**"40 units in stock. 75 units ordered. Who gets fulfilled?"**  
When inventory runs scarce, gut-feeling allocation costs millions in lost tier-1 accounts and chargebacks. OrderWise replaces spreadsheet chaos with mathematical certainty: AI analyzes business context, deterministic rules allocate inventory down to the unit, and authorized humans approve every decision.

## Hook (first 4.5 seconds)
- **Visual:** Dark industrial atmosphere (`#08090D`) with sweeping animated red mesh waves (`#E11D32`).
- **Text:** 
  - *"40 Units in Stock."*
  - *"75 Units Ordered."*
  - *"Who gets fulfilled?"*
- **Graphic:** Pressure Gauge Pro (`GAUGE-P300`) with glowing inventory indicator showing 40 units on hand versus 3 competing enterprise customer orders.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Deterministic Rule Engine (4.5s – 9.5s):**
   - 3D Isometric Warehouse Facility with animated conveyor.
   - 3-step hierarchy: Approved Priority (Critical > High > Med > Low) → Promised Delivery Date → Creation Timestamp.
   - Live queue resolving: Charlie (30/30 Fulfilled), Alice (Deferred - below 15 minimum), Bob (Deferred - partial not permitted).
2. **AI Priority Assistant with Human Governance (9.5s – 14.5s):**
   - Groq Llama-3 AI extracts evidence from emergency order notes.
   - Recommends CRITICAL priority with confidence score.
   - Strict human approval gate before database commitment.
3. **What-If Simulation Lab & Impact Analyzer (14.5s – 19.5s):**
   - Live run comparison diff showing zero-leakage simulation.
   - Scenario: `+50 units emergency replenishment`.
   - Alice switches from `Deferred → Fulfilled`, protecting key account revenue.
